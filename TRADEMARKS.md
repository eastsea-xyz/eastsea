## What you may do without asking

- Use, copy, modify and redistribute the code under its licenses.
- Say truthfully what your software is and how it relates to ours, for
  example "compatible with EastSea", "a wallet for the EastSea network" or
  "a fork of EastSea", in plain text and without our logos.
- Write, report and teach about the project by name.

## What you may not do

- Name a fork, chain, token, wallet, exchange listing, domain or app with our
  marks or a name confusingly similar to them.
- Present your software, network or token as the official one, or as endorsed
  by, affiliated with or operated by Pipln when it is not.
- Use our logos or app icons.

If you fork the code and run your own network, choose your own name. Which
network is the official one is decided by the genesis it starts from and the
keys it trusts, and is published in this repository's release notes.
