What you get

Built for the Mac,
trusted by you.

Your thumb is the key

No 12-word seed phrase. Your key is born inside the Secure Enclave and never leaves it. Every payment asks for Touch ID — nothing to write down, nothing to lose.

Your Mac checks the books

The wallet never asks a server for your balance. It verifies the network's threshold signature and state proof right on your Mac — reading the ledger, not trusting a screen.

One switch, network participation

Turn on the node switch and you're done. No VPN, no open ports — it connects over your home internet. While it's online it participates in the network and receives the protocol's uptime reward.

An allowance wallet for AI agents

Hand Claude Code, Codex and other MCP agents a wallet of their own. You set the caps, recipients and expiry with Touch ID — the chain itself enforces every limit.
