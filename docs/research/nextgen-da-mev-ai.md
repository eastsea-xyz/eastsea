## Recommendations

**A) DA - build now:** Sovereign rollup on Celestia via Lumina (Rust, wasm, phone-capable light node) is the best match for "ZK blocks verified by laptops/phones". At ~$0.019/MB it is roughly half Ethereum blob cost with far simpler integration. Keep a thin Ethereum-blob adapter behind a trait so you can settle later if bridge/liquidity demands it. **Wait:** Avail/EigenDA as alternates; based rollups/Espresso are irrelevant to a self-sequenced chain.

**B) MEV - build now:** (1) FOCIL-style inclusion lists from a rotating validator committee (cheap, no crypto). (2) Commonware DKG + existing `bls12381::tle` for per-height timelock, Shutter-Gnosis semantics (decrypt at commit, 1-block delay). (3) Priority-fee burn. **Wait:** batched threshold encryption (Commonware constructions unshipped, tradeoff triangle unresolved) and Ethereum "Lucid" spec; adopt BTE when a commonware crate lands (likely 2027).

**C) AI - build now:** tiny CPU classifiers (tract or candle) for peer scoring, tx conflict hints for Block-STM pre-scheduling, proof-queue prioritization, all advisory and locally overridable. Expose CoreML/MLX only through an optional feature flag. **Wait:** deterministic/consensus-safe ML (requires integer-only kernels; research still finding cross-kernel divergence), and zkML/opML (cost 10^4x, not needed for advisors). For deterministic parallel scheduling, prefer Block-level Access Lists over any learned predictor.
