Subject: EastSea: a public network experiment on Macs, for critique

I've been working on EastSea: can ordinary Macs run and prove a public
network with no owner? Voting admission is gated, and founder reserves
can hold a bootstrap quorum. [1]

The paper covers drawn Simplex BFT, threshold BLS, asynchronous Jolt/Akita
proofs on Metal, certified snapshots and pruning. Followers and wallets
trust committee certification of block proofs; they do not verify them
independently. Proofs can lag or be absent. [1]

A 2026-10-09 regional simulation recorded conditional empirical p99
finality of 0.699002 seconds across 236 completed proposals at 16 seats,
20 Mbps shared upload and roughly 50 KB payloads. [2] A 2026-09-27 warm
Metal sample of fifty payments took 47.2 seconds on an M1 Max. [3]

This is testnet; updates retain operating keys. Early, unproven,
without an independent security audit. Please don't
participate expecting money. I'd welcome assumption challenges, code/paper
review, failure reports and measurements from your Mac. [1]

Try a disposable local devnet (resets the directory; no public voting seat).
Participation uses your hardware and electricity at your cost.
