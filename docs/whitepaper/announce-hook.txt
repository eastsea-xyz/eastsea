Subject: Can the Mac on your desk help keep a public network alive?

A 2026-09-27 warm Metal sample of fifty payments took 47.2 seconds to prove
on an M1 Max. Finality precedes execution proofs. Is that a trade you'd accept? [1]

I started EastSea to test a question: can ordinary Macs run and prove a
public network with no owner? Voting admission is gated, and founder reserves
can hold a bootstrap quorum. Followers and wallets trust committee certification
of block proofs; they do not independently verify them. [2]

The paper separates testnet evidence from proposals: Simplex/BLS finality,
Jolt/Akita proofs, certified snapshots and pruning. Today's network is testnet.
Proofs can lag or be absent;
software releases retain operating authorities. Early, unproven, without an
independent audit. Please don't join expecting money. [2]

A 2026-10-09 regional simulation recorded conditional empirical p99 finality
of 0.699002 seconds across 236 completed proposals at 16 seats, 20 Mbps
shared upload and roughly 50 KB payloads. Conditions below; home-Mac results
remain unestablished. [3]
