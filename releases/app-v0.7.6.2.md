# EastSea 0.7.6.2 (testnet)

EastSea 0.7.6.2 for macOS (Apple silicon), signed by Pipln and notarized by Apple.

Drag the app to Applications. The app is a wallet and, with the switch on, a node that verifies every block on this Mac. It connects to the EastSea testnet (test tokens have no value).

Updates arrive automatically (EastSea ▸ Check for Updates…).

What's new:
- Node rewards stay on one line. Long amounts such as today's reward were split across two lines on the home screen; amounts in the reward card, the sidebar and the menu bar now show up to four decimals on one line, and the exact amount appears when you point at it.
- The globe no longer leaves a large empty box. For the first minutes after the node starts, the network keeps its connection count private until enough Macs have been seen; the app now shows a short line in a small card during that time instead of an empty globe panel, and the globe with the number of Macs appears as soon as the count is public.
